---
name: survive-first-night
description: Step-by-step plan for getting through the first Minecraft night. Load when starting a new world or when the player asks how to stay safe at night.
---

Daytime lasts about 10 real minutes. Work through these steps in order:

1. Punch trees for at least 10 logs.
2. Craft planks, then a crafting table, then sticks.
3. Craft a wooden pickaxe, mine about 20 cobblestone, then craft a stone pickaxe, sword, and axe.
4. Kill 3 sheep for wool and craft a bed. A bed lets you skip the night.
5. Collect coal from exposed stone and craft torches.
6. Before sunset, dig into a hillside or build a small hut. Seal the entrance and light the inside with torches.
7. If you have no bed, stay inside until morning. Do not wander in the dark without armor.
