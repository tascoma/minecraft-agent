---
name: the-end
description: How to find the stronghold and get ready for the End and the ender dragon. Load when the player talks about the End, the stronghold, eyes of ender or the dragon.
---

1. Eyes of ender: each is a blaze powder (a blaze rod makes 2) and an ender pearl. The End portal needs up to 12, plus a few to find the stronghold, so aim for about 15. `make_item` "ender_eye" makes them once you have the rods and pearls (see nether).
2. Finding the stronghold: `throw_ender_eye` in the Overworld. It says which way the eye flew; walk a few hundred blocks that way together and throw again. When the eye goes straight down, the stronghold is right below: dig down carefully.
3. Inside, find the portal room (a lava pool under a frame of 12 end portal blocks) and fill the empty frames with eyes.
4. Before going in: full iron or diamond armor, a bow and plenty of arrows (the dragon flies; the end crystals on the pillars heal it), lots of food, blocks for pillaring, and beds are no use. Going in is one way until the dragon is dead, so check with the player first.
